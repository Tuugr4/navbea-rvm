The original yolov5n-seg.pt checkpoint is exported to dynamic FP32 ONNX,
opset 12. Source, checkpoint, export script and pinned hashes are included
with the corresponding-source distribution. No current Ultralytics Python
package is used or shipped. Selection and masking are implemented locally
in worker/subjects.py. No biometric identity is stored.
